# Sujud for Windows Privacy Policy

_Effective October 29, 2026_

Sujud does not collect or share any personal data.

**Location.** Sujud calculates prayer times and the Qibla direction for your location. It uses your PC's location if you allow location access in Windows, or the coordinates you enter if you don't. The calculation happens entirely on your PC. The location is saved only on your PC, so the times are ready when you open the app or look at a widget. Sujud never sends it anywhere.

**Windows location services.** When you allow location access, Windows works out where your PC is. Microsoft's privacy statement applies to Windows location services.

**Widgets.** Widgets you add show prayer times on the Widgets board and the lock screen. Their content is created on your PC and passed only to Windows to display.

**Notifications.** Prayer time notifications are scheduled locally on your PC.

**Settings.** The calculation method and other choices you make are kept on your PC.

**No accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking.** Depending on your Windows diagnostic data settings, Microsoft may share crash reports with the developer through the Microsoft Store.
